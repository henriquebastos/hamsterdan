# 1. Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss code and ideas without personal
attacks. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention, and
publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to
address harmful conduct. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to henrique@bastos.net. Include links and
enough context to explain the incident, while limiting personal information to
what is necessary. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
