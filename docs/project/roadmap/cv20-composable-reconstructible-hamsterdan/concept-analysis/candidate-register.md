row records evidence, not accepted language. Definitions remain provisional
until the candidate is traced through DS13 and accepted into the project

## DS12 disposition

DS12 introduces no new production-domain concept. Journeys, overlays,
correspondence rows, fixtures, shrinking, coverage and the qualification report
are evidence vocabulary. They qualify DS1–DS11 meanings but cannot redefine
them or hide a late production capability.

## DS13 final filter

DS13 makes **Hamsterdan** the only canonical product and implementation name.
Stable operation identity, fresh-root reconstruction, authority and supervision
survive. Opaque rollback custody and the no-return point are cutover safeguards,
not necessarily product glossary concepts. Temporary generation labels,
selectors, migration, compatibility and cutover APIs do not survive.
