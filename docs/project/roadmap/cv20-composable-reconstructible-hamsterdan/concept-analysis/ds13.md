## Extracted behavior

DS13 applies the final-language filter. After explicit approval, the old service
is fenced, old state becomes bounded opaque rollback custody, and the qualified
system starts on fresh roots under canonical package, service, state and
operator names. The first accepted external mutation is the no-return point:
after it, rollback requires reconciliation by stable operation identity and can
never downgrade schemas. The completed product and implementation are simply
Hamsterdan; no generation selector or compatibility lane remains.

## Earlier concepts completed or refined

- **Hamsterdan:** DS13 makes it the product and only canonical implementation;
  temporary generation labels cease to be active truth.
- **Operation:** stable logical identity remains the reconciliation key across
  service fencing, in-flight work and any post-no-return rollback.
- **Reconstruction:** canonical Hamsterdan reconstructs only from fresh canonical
  roots; it never reads or translates old state.
- **Authority and supervision:** service stop/fence precedes replacement, and
  canonical startup is qualified under bounded supervision before mutation.
- **Every DS1–DS12 candidate:** only meanings expressible without V5,
  Hamsterdan2, migration, compatibility or replacement-selection language
  survive this story.

## Subordinate vocabulary to evaluate

- Opaque rollback custody persists after cutover until separately approved
  deletion, but it is an operational safeguard rather than product behavior.
- No-return point names the first externally accepted mutation during cutover;
  it may remain in operational history rather than the product glossary.
- Fresh roots are a final-state invariant of canonical Hamsterdan, not a
  long-lived migration concept.
- Canonical package, service, state and operator names are manifestations of
  **Hamsterdan**, not separate concepts.

## Unresolved DS-review items

- preparation, inspection, stop, snapshot, verify, start and reconcile command
  names/arguments;
- destructive-command dry-run and confirmation behavior;
- cutover plan, preflight, journal and final evidence schemas;
- snapshot identity, integrity, access, retention and restore contracts;
- no-return evidence and approved external-operation fixture;
- active-name/path/schema census and failure format;
- rollback commands and decisions on either side of no-return; and
- removal of temporary cutover command names after completion.

All are cutover APIs. Their DS review cannot create a permanent version selector
or compatibility concept.

## DS13 final-language exclusions

- `hamsterdan2`, Hamsterdan2, V5, `host/v5`, `readiness/net_v5`, `pr_v5`,
  `build_net_v5`, `V5*`, topology selectors and topology-only operation prefixes.
- Replacement, old/current implementation, non-selectable runtime, shadow
  runtime, dual runtime and generation-selection language.
- Old ingress, timer, review, binding, route, runnable, catalog, Dispatch and
  History schemas or identities; migration, downgrade and compatibility readers.
- `contracts`, `ready.facts`, migration folds/arcs, retired result aliases,
  qualification values and Petrus DST/profile compatibility identities.
- Preparation commands, action journals, censuses, path moves, gate promotion,
  snapshots and no-return fixtures as permanent product concepts.
- Historical CV17/CV18/ES and worklog terminology as an authority for current
  language. It remains provenance only.
