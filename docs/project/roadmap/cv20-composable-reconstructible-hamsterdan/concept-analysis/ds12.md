## Extracted behavior

DS12 introduces no new production behavior. It qualifies the complete system by
running twelve declared journeys and their applicable recovery overlays through
real production owners and deterministic composition. Each material simulation
claim is paired with direct real-seam evidence. The result is one bounded report
bound to the exact revision, dependencies, fixtures, limits, resources and
outcomes for the separate DS13 decision. Any production gap returns to its owning
tracer instead of becoming a hidden DS12 capability.

## Earlier concepts completed or refined

- **All DS1–DS11 candidates:** DS12 traces them through composed journeys and
  recovery overlays but does not alter their definitions.
- **Local checker and cross checker:** DS12 combines their reports and verifies
  sensitivity; they remain technical evidence concepts, never runtime authority.
- **Exact replay and reconstruction:** fresh-object replay and fresh-process
  correspondence jointly test the distinction between simulation and durable
  recovery.
- **Operation and occurrence:** journey declarations pin stable identities and
  physical effect cardinality across faults.
- **Authority, lifecycle, timers, discovery and fairness:** required overlays
  show these concepts compose with applicable user journeys rather than living
  only in isolated tests.

## Subordinate vocabulary to evaluate

- Qualification report, correspondence row, fixture, expected observation,
  known limit and unavailable result belong to evidence reporting.
- Journey, schedule and overlay are qualification organization terms, not
  necessarily final product concepts.
- “Same semantic violation”, semantic shrinking and coverage accounting are
  test contracts.
- The twelve journey names are named cuts through accepted behavior, not twelve
  new glossary concepts.
- Revision, dependency and fixture identity are report bindings.

## Unresolved DS-review items

- expectation DSL for setup, input, cuts, restart, effects, posture and bounds;
- shrinking and same-semantic-violation contracts;
- local, cross, resource, coverage and portfolio report schemas;
- correspondence pass/fail/unavailable vocabulary;
- subprocess and physical-attempt observation protocols;
- revision/dependency/artifact bindings and evidence limits; and
- ordinary-CI versus separately approved live-qualification commands.

## DS13 final-language exclusions

- The non-selectable replacement, pre-cutover census and DS13 decision report
  are delivery machinery, not final-system concepts.
- Journey names, schedules, shrinkers, checker reports, fixtures, coverage and
  correspondence rows are qualification evidence.
- Source/wheel checks, process kills, mock transport and separately approved live
  runs qualify behavior but do not define it.
- DS12 cannot add a new effect, custody model, authority policy or concurrency
  dimension; any such item belongs to its owning tracer and must not survive as
  a DS12-derived concept.
