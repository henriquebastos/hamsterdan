## Extracted behavior

DS10 recovers eligible open pull requests whose first webhook was not retained.
Host traverses only operator-configured repositories through a bounded durable
discovery pass. A list page supplies candidates, not workflow truth. Every
unknown candidate receives an exact provider read before idempotent subject
registration and runnable enqueue into the known-subject reconciliation path.
A persisted pass boundary supports restart without claiming an atomic repository
snapshot or catalog completeness.

## Earlier concepts completed or refined

- **PR subject and subject registration:** DS10 adds the missing-webhook route by
  which an unknown eligible PR becomes one registered subject.
- **Observation acquisition and admission:** list summaries never enter History;
  the exact read reuses DS2's snapshot/provenance and common admission seam.
- **Reconstruction/recovery:** DS9 reconciles known subjects. DS10 extends
  recovery to unknown eligible open PRs in configured repositories.
- **Cut:** list-page, candidate-classification and pass-completion boundaries are
  new host cut instances, not new cut concepts.
- **Runnable work:** successful registration includes an idempotent enqueue; DS11
  later completes fair scheduling of discovery and known-subject turns.

## Subordinate vocabulary to evaluate

- `RepositoryDiscoveryPassBoundary` is the persisted resume boundary inside a
  discovery pass, not a generic cursor.
- `LastCompletedOpenPullRequestDiscoveryPass` is inspection/scheduling evidence,
  not a completeness watermark.
- `UnknownPullRequestDiscovery` identifies acquisition provenance and may belong
  under observation provenance rather than stand alone.
- Discovery candidate, provider page, continuation, rate reserve and defer
  window are mechanics within bounded discovery.
- “Configured repository” may be a qualifier on discovery scope rather than a
  separate domain concept.

## DS13 final-language exclusions

- Discovery remains a recovery capability, but pre-cutover “replacement” and
  non-selectability language disappears.
- Provider pages, cursors/timestamps, ETags and named discovery cuts are API or
  transport mechanics, not standalone final-system concepts.
- Generated races, process death, DST schedules and GitHub fixtures are
  qualification evidence.
- Any wording that treats a completed pass as an atomic snapshot, a completeness
  watermark or lifecycle authority is excluded by the final contract.
