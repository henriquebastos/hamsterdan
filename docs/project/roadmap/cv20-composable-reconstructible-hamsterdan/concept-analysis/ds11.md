## Extracted behavior

DS11 composes independent one-PR readiness lifecycles into bounded host
supervision. A durable catalog locates subjects and roots. Runnable custody uses
durable enqueue order, eligibility, leases, coalesced reasons and tail requeue to
select one known-subject or repository-discovery turn fairly. Each service turn
opens one lifecycle, invokes readiness at most once, persists consequences and
requeues or closes. Startup, inspection and shutdown remain bounded and isolate
one subject's failure from unrelated progress.

## Earlier concepts completed or refined

- **Readiness lifecycle:** DS11 keeps one lifecycle per PR and calls it at most
  once per host turn; host does not absorb workflow or readiness meaning.
- **PR subject and readiness root:** the instance catalog generalizes DS1's one
  immutable binding into bounded portfolio lookup without copying workflow state.
- **Work posture:** host persists bounded wake/deadline consequences and uses
  detached posture for inspection, never as workflow authority.
- **Configured-repository discovery:** DS10's discovery turns join the same fair
  scheduling discipline as known-subject turns without entering readiness.
- **Wake hint:** DS8's deadline/wake hint can be lost and reconstructed from
  catalog plus readiness inspection.
- **Cut:** one service turn preserves the nested readiness cut unchanged.

## Subordinate vocabulary to evaluate

- Enqueue sequence, eligibility instant, selection lease, coalesced reasons and
  tail requeue are likely the internal vocabulary of runnable custody.
- Known-subject turn and discovery turn are work variants governed by one fair
  supervision concept.
- Service turn may be useful subordinate vocabulary for the one-selection,
  one-readiness-call unit.
- Portfolio inspection and catalog page are bounded views, not sources of
  workflow authority.
- Startup, shutdown, operator/API rims and qualification commands support
  supervision but are not peer domain concepts.

## DS13 final-language exclusions

- “Replacement service”, disabled/non-selectable posture and temporary package
  paths end at cutover; final language says Hamsterdan service.
- FastAPI lifespan, CLI method names, lease cut names and store transactions are
  API or process mechanics, not standalone concepts.
- Generated schedules, substitution checks, process-kill fixtures and physical
  counts are qualification evidence.
- In-memory queue semantics, hidden readiness drains and host interpretation of
  workflow state are explicitly excluded from the final system.
