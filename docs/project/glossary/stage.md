# Stage

The coarse answer to "where is this PR's workflow right now", such as awaiting observation.

- Use when: a reported label for humans and callers.
- Do not use for: stored truth or decisions; the PR's history is the source of truth and no decision rests on a stage alone. Also not for the nine subnets.
- Avoid: posture.
- Related: [PR Workflow](pr-workflow.md), [Checkpoint](checkpoint.md)
